▶Night Flight 5K and 10K Trail Run — 8:30 am, Saturday, April 4, Mifflinburg

An exciting nighttime race featuring both 5K and 10K options. Runners can decide at registration whether they want to complete a single 5K loop or take on the challenge of the 10K by running a double loop. This course is set on the Dandelion 5K trail—run in reverse for an extra twist! No changes at the start line or during the race. Runners are strongly encouraged to bring their own light source, such as a headlamp, flashlight, or torch, as the course will not provide these. The trail will be marked with glow sticks and reflective arrows to guide participants safely through the dark. After crossing the finish line, enjoy delicious post-race food and a peanut butter egg to celebrate your run!

  355 Mt Luther Lane, Camp Mount Luther, Mifflinburg, PA

▶10K Run/Walk for the Osprey — 9 am, Sunday, April 19, Lake Ariel

The ospreys are back! Each spring, these incredible raptors (Pandion haliaetus) return to Lacawac from their tropical wintering grounds—and we’re celebrating their arrival with our Spring 10K trail run and 5K walk! Lace up for a scenic race through Lacawac’s trails and landscapes, all while supporting habitat conservation. Don’t miss the chance to run alongside nature and welcome back our resident osprey, nesting high above Heron Pond near the Education Center. Proceeds will benefit the upkeep and maintenance of Lacawac's Browning Beaver Meadow Sanctuary, a former sanctuary of the NEPA Audubon Society.

Parking: Lacawac Sanctuary has limited parking, so please consider carpooling.

Awards:

  • 10K Trail Run: Overall 1st, 2nd and 3rd male and female (cash awards), plus top three finishers in age groups: 0-14, 15-19, 20-29, 30-39, 40-49, 50-59, 60-69, 70+
  • 5K Walk: Non-competitive

 182 Sanctuary Road, Lake Ariel, PA (41.3763667, −75.2996667)
