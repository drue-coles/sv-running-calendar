▶Levee Up 2.5-Mile/5-Mile Race — 2 pm, Sunday, March 8, Danville

The waters that run through the Danville Borough have created quite a roar through history. Several floods have run wild through the borough, causing the townspeople to seek protection and build a Levee system. Race this mostly grassy loop and take notice of the water that has carved its path and made its way over the banks into the borough on many occasions through history. Not Today, Agnes! Today we Levee Up and rise above.
