▶Watsontown JesVic 5K Run/Walk — 9 am, Saturday, June 6, Watsontown

The course winds through the streets of Watsontown and Delaware Township with great views of the Susquehanna River Valley. There is also a Kid's 1/2-mile race at 10:20 am. Registration and awards are at the Warrior Run Area Fire Department located across the street from the Watsontown Elementary School.

The Watsontown JesVic 5K was started in 2007 by Shannon (Hoffman) Pyles and Suzie (Wade) Watts to honor two community women, Jessica Wade and Vicki Hitesman, who both passed away from cancer. Proceeds from the race supports local families with members facing disease and struggling with the resulting medical bills. More than $60,000 has been raised with the support of sponsor businesses, donations, volunteers, runners, walkers, spectators and friends.

Questions? Contact the race director at jesvic5k@gmail.com.

  1125 Main Street, Watsontown, PA

▶Rattlesnake Run 5K Run and One-Mile Walk — 8 am, Saturday, June 13, Lehigh Township

The course is flat and fast, out-and-back, on a crushed gravel trail in Lehigh Gorge State Park, which parallels the Lehigh River for about 20 miles. The 5K run starts at 8 am and the one-mile fun walk starts at 9. All proceeds benefit the Carbon County Environmental Education Center (CCEEC), which provides hands-on nature-based programs for thousands of school students and other visitors each year. It is also a licensed wildlife rehabilitation center, caring for injured and orphaned wild animals found by the public. The Rattlesnake Run is the largest annual fundraiser for the CCEEC.

Awards: First three overall male and female receive repurposed industrial chain “rattlesnakes” on stone bases. Age category awards are repurposed bicycle chain “rattlesnakes” on wooden bases.

  Glen Onoko Access to Lehigh Gorge State Park, Lehigh Township, PA (40.8835, -75.75914)

▶Chris Shultz Memorial Run Walk— Sunday, June 28, Millville, PA

On January 27, 2010, Chris Shultz lost her battle with leukemia at 40 years of age. A native of Millville, PA, she left behind not only a young family and dear friends but a whole community that felt the loss. As a teacher, she inspired many to find their best in a spirit of safe, positive fun. Friends of Chris seeks to continue that atmosphere as we raise funds for The Leukemia and Lymphoma Society, and Camp Courage. Please join us for a great time for these two great causes.

Start the holiday by celebrating the life of Chris Shultz and raising money to battle leukemia, and support grieving children through Camp Courage! There are many ways to participate – hit the course with a spirit of fun competition and run it with a clocked result, take it easy, walk it and enjoy the scenery, cheer from the sidelines, or swim at the pool.

  200 North Chestnut Street, Little Fishing Creek Area Swimming Pool, Millville, PA
