▶Lisa Napoletano Running with the Angels 5K and 1M — 9 am, Saturday, May 9, Jim Thorpe

Lisa Napoletano was a Freshman at Marian and a member of the Cross Country and Track and Field teams. Early March, she was involved in an unfortunate ATV accident and passed away from her injuries. The Marian Community, Cross Country Team, and Track and Field Team was blessed to know her and have her in our lives, even for such a short period of time. Part of this event is dedicated to her memory and the impact she had on her school and her teams. In order to honor her, her family has set up a scholarship fund and non-profit organization "Lisa Napoletano-Running with the Angels." The scholarship is awarded to a member of the Cross Country Team, Track and Field Team, or is active in snowboarding and display strong team spirit and values academics.

Events: 5K run, 5K walk, Youth 1-Mile race, 1-mile adult fun run, and virtual 5K/1M.

▶Outrun the Darkness 9.88K, 5K, Kids' Fun Run and 1 Mile Memory Walk — 7pm, Saturday, May 16, Hazleton

In the United States, a person dies by suicide every 12.3 minutes. That is 132 people per day and half a million people per year. Luzerne County made up 54 of those deaths in 2024. It is also the leading cause of death among young people aged 10-24. To address some of these statistics, the Friends of Rachel Club is excited to announce our 2nd Annual Suicide Prevention and Awareness Race. This event is a pivotal part of our mission to raise awareness about suicide prevention, promote mental health, and foster a supportive community for those affected by suicide.

We are currently offering a 9.88K (in reference to the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline number), 5K, 1 Mile Memory Walk/Run and Kid’s Fun Run. Although event day is being held at the Hazleton Area High School Track & Field, runners registered for the 5K and 9.88K will not run their respective races around the track but the Hazleton Area High School campus and nearby.

  Hazleton Area High School, 1400 West 23rd Street, Hazleton, PA
