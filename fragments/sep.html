▶4th annual Wyoming Valley Run — 8 am, Sunday, September 6, Pittston

This 10-mile run stretches from Pittston to Wilkes-Barre, featuring some of the Valley’s best views along the way. You’ll start in downtown Pittston and cross over the Susquehanna River into West Pittston. From there, it's a straight shot down Wyoming Avenue through Exeter, Wyoming, Forty Fort, and Kingston. The route takes a left turn onto Market Street, where you can see the Wilkes-Barre skyline in the distance. You’ll cross over the Market Street Bridge and make your way to the finish line at Public Square in downtown Wilkes-Barre. The course is fun and approachable for runners of all levels, whether you’re chasing down a PR or striving for your first double-digit race. Runners, walkers, and handcyclists are welcome.

The race expo will take place on Saturday, September 5, from 12 to 5 PM at Mohegan Pennsylvania (1280 PA-315, Wilkes-Barre, PA 18702). Race participants are required to pick up their race packets at the expo during this time. The expo is open to the public, with expected attendance in excess of 2,000 visitors.

Community is at the heart of The Wyoming Valley Run. Since 2023, the race has donated over $35,000 to local nonprofits. See the race website for details.

  There are complimentary race shuttles for participants in downtown Wilkes-Barre to the starting line in Kingston (details).

▶Phillips Phun Run 5K — 9 am, Saturday, September 5, Mount Carmel

Starting and finishing at the Mount Carmel Area Jr/Sr High School, the course will take participants on a loop through the streets and trails in and around Mount Carmel. The course features a variety of different landscapes and sites that are unique to the community. There will be a FREE kids run around the school parking lot. Proceeds benefit the Mount Carmel Area Community Center, a safe gathering place for the social, cultural and educational needs of the community.

The Phillips Phun Run is a proud member of the Anthracite Running Series.

  Mount Carmel High School, 600 West 5th Street, Mount Carmel, PA
