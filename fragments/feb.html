▶County to County 5K — 2 pm, Sunday, February 15, Danville

Start at the FQ Hartman Park Area in Montour County and dash straight down Front Street to the Danville/Riverside Bridge. Take a right and cross the bridge that stretches across the Susquehanna River, otherwise known as “Muddy Waters.” You enter Northumberland County halfway across the bridge. Take another right and run down the D&H Avenue to the Merck Cherokee Plant. Make a left around a grassy area of a soccer field and green space, and head back on the Avenue D&H and retrace your steps back to Montour County and the FQ Hartman Park Area.

The scenery is amazing, and no matter the weather, you can’t help but feel the excitement of running through the counties and crossing paths with the other race participants (high fives welcome).

  Senior Citizen Center, 500 West Mahoning Street, Danville, PA

▶The Big Runaway Mile — 2 pm, Sunday, February 22, Danville

Inspired by a journey for safety along the Susquehanna River, the settlers fled from the Native Indians and the British (1778). Run this one-mile race as fast as you can! Starting at the FQ Harman Park Area, get off the start line Fast and don’t look back. You will make a quick turn at the half-mile point and see your competition on the way back to the Finish Line!

  Senior Citizen Center, 500 West Mahoning Street, Danville, PA
