▶Hellbender 5K and Half Marathon — 8 am, Sunday, August 23, Bear Gap

This is a great course for both road and trail runners. Fast, flat, out-and-back along the resevoir lakes in beautiful Weiser State Forest. The Half Marathon starts at 8 am and the 5K starts at 8:30. Proceeds benefit the Roaring Creek Valley Conservation Association.

The race gets its name from the species of aquatic salamander known as the hellbender, which is native to the hills and mountains of the eastern U.S. and can grow up to two feet long. Because they breathe through their skin, they require healthy, pristine, unpolluted streams and creeks to survive. Due to development and urbanization over recent decades, however, many of their habitats have become fouled and their numbers have drastically declined—they’re just one of the many species the race organizers hope to benefit over the long term with their efforts here.

  Route 54 Parking Area - Weiser State Forest, Roaring Creek Tract, Bear Gap, PA

▶Salt Springs Field, Forest and Falls Trail Races — Saturday, August 29, Montrose

The rugged trail race you know and love is back for its 14th year in 2026! Participants can choose from a 5K, 10K or 50K option. All courses are technical trails through a beautiful old-growth forest with spectacular scenery. The 5K will feature a course that is almost entirely on trail. The 10K course includes a flat, fast section featuring the beautiful Wetlands trail as you run past fields and streams. Don't worry, you'll still get your 3 steep climbs, technical descents and stunning sights. For those who really want a challenge, register for the 50K. This gnarly and beautiful course will be 5 loops on the 10K course, totaling 7,000+ feet of elevation gain. Want to read more about the 50K (or any of the races, really)? Check out this review by Inaugural Overall Winner Cole Crosby! All three courses will throw a little bit of everything at you: steep climbs, fast runnable single track, and technical descents. This event is true PA trail running. All races will be chip timed by Falcon Race Timing. Stick around after your race to enjoy food, beverages and camaraderie.
