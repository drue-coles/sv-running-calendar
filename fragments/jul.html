▶Viaduct Trail Ultramarathon — 5 am, Saturday-Sunday, July 11-12, Lanesboro

Pennsylvania's oldest 100-mile footrace. You can enter to run 50 or 100 official miles (70 runners per race maximum). Beginning in Luciana Park, by the Starrucca Viaduct, the course is a somewhat challenging series of out-and-back laps, some of which is stony, unimproved rail trail. Although four-wheeler traffic has made two nice gravel tracks along much of the course, rocks are impossible to avoid in some places. Because of the rocky sections, decent trail-running shoes are probably the best option. Gaiters can help to keep out the gravel and cinders.

Prerequisites:

  • 50-mile entrants must have previously completed a footrace of a marathon distance or longer.
  • 100-mile entrants must have completed a footrace of 50K or longer.
  • All entrants must be at least 18 years old.

Entry Fee: $0.00. That's right, nothing. We like to think of our race one of those priceless experiences at the end of a long list of prior investments. Those who wish to donate to the other VTU runners should bring some aid-station food or drinks to share.

Awards: Each runner who finishes the distance for which he or she registered earns a railroad spike. The first male and female finishers for each distance get a silver-painted one.

Registration: If you understand and agree with the waiver information on the race website's Registration page and all of the other information on this site, and you still inexplicably wish to enter, email us the following information before the Fourth of July:

  1. Your name
  2. The race distance you wish to run (50 or 100 miles)
  3. Your age on July 11, 2026
  4. Your gender
  5. Your hometown and state
  6. A phone number, preferably a cell taht you will carry on-course
  7. A web link to your qualifying race results

  Luciana Park, Lanesboro, PA (41.965349,-75.582744)
