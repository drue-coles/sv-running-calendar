Coaching

▶Tina Bartholomew (Danville)

Tina Bartholomew offers personalized running coaching for all ages and experience levels, including beginners, first-time runners, athletes returning after time off, runners training with or without a race goal, and anyone looking for accountability and community. Tina has coached cross country and track and field at the collegiate, middle school, and high school levels. As an athlete at Bloomsburg University, she qualified for the national meet in the 5000 meters and was the first female track and field athlete in the history of the school to compete at the NCAA Division II national championships. Her performances in the 3000- and 5000-meter events rank among the program's top ten all-time.

Reach out to Tina directly for coaching here, or visit the Run Free Running Club for more information.
