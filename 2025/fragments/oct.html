▶12th annual Lake to Lake 8K Trail Run, 5K Woods Walk, and Dog Wag — 9 am, Sunday, October 19, Lake Ariel

Lacawac’s Lake to Lake 8k Trail Run, 5k Woods Walk, and Dog Wag is a fall fan favorite for runners, walkers, and nature lovers alike. This scenic autumn race invites participants to run or walk a course that winds through 550-acres of protected forests and wetlands, traversing between Lake Lacawac and Lake Wallenpaupack. The course follows well-marked trails that allow participants to fully experience the beauty of Lacawac in the fall. Whether you are aiming for a new personal best, enjoying a morning walk with family and friends, or bringing along a four-legged companion, this autumn race is perfect for anyone who enjoys friendly competition in the great outdoors.

Awards:

  • 8K Trail Run: Overall 1st, 2nd and 3rd male and female (cash awards), plus top three finishers in age groups: 0-14, 15-19, 20-29, 30-39, 40-49, 50-59, 60-69, 70+
  • Family and Dog Woods Walk: Non-competitive

Parking: Lacawac Sanctuary has limited parking, so please consider carpooling.

 94 Sanctuary Road, Lake Ariel, PA (41°22.582′ N, 75°17.980′ W)
