▶Red, White, and Run 5K — 9 am, Saturday, November 15, Lackawaxen

Making its debut this year, Red, White, and Run 5K is more than a race — it’s a tribute. Held in honor of Veterans Day, this event brings our community together to celebrate freedom, service, and sacrifice. Run or walk with purpose and show your support for the heroes among us. Participants can choose from three great options:

  • Timed 5K Run — A competitive race with official timing for runners of all levels.
  • 5K Walk — A non-competitive option for those who prefer to enjoy the course at their own pace.
  • Fun Walk — A shorter, family-friendly walk around Veteran’s Memorial Park, perfect for all ages.

Awards:

  • 5K Run: Overall 1st, 2nd and 3rd male and female (cash awards), plus top three finishers in age groups: 0-14, 15-19, 20-29, 30-39, 40-49, 50-59, 60-69, 70+
  • 5K Walk or Fun Run: Non-competitive

 Veteran's Memorial Park, 109 Scenic Drive, Lackawaxen, PA
