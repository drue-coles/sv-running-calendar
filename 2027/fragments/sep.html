▶Dandelion Trail Race — 9 am, Saturday, September 4, Mifflinburg

The Dandelion Trail Race is a welcoming, entry-level trail event offering both 5K and 10K options to benefit Camp Mount Luther. Whether you're a runner, hiker, walker, or new to the trails, this event is a great opportunity to enjoy the outdoors at your own pace. Participants can decide on race day whether to complete a single 5K loop or take on the 10K by completing two loops of the course. A water stop is available at the 5K mark for participants continuing onto their second loop.

Named in memory of Rev. Glenn Heasley, who had a fondness for dandelions, this event celebrates his spirit while bringing the community together in a beautiful, natural setting. Stick around after the race and enjoy a light breakfast featuring bagels, fruit, and cookies while connecting with fellow trail enthusiasts. Come run, hike, or walk with us, enjoy the trails, and support a great cause. All paces are welcome.

  355 Mount Luther Lane, Camp Mount Luther, Mifflinburg, PA

▶The Dam Half and Dam 4-Mile Ish — 8 am, Sunday, September 26, Mifflinburg

The Dam Half Trail Challenge has grown from a small race for hardcore trail runners into a popular, family-friendly event that welcomes hundreds of participants each year. Whether you're a runner, hiker, or walker, we invite you to experience the scenic trails and be part of our trail community. Stick around after crossing the finish line to enjoy light snacks, music, and time with friends and fellow trail enthusiasts.

  R.B. Winter State Park, Mifflinburg, PA
