▶Night Flight Trail Race — 8:30 am, Saturday, April 3, Mifflinburg

The Night Flight Trail Race is an exciting nighttime trail event offering both 5K and 10K options. Runners, hikers, and walkers of all experience levels are welcome to enjoy this unique adventure under the stars. Participants select their distance at registration, choosing between a single 5K loop or the 10K challenge of completing two loops. The course follows the Dandelion 5K trail in reverse, adding an extra twist to the nighttime adventure. Distance changes are not permitted at the starting line or during the event. Stick around after the event to enjoy light snacks and connect with fellow trail enthusiasts. Every participant will also receive a delicious chocolate egg from a local establishment as a special treat.

Participants are strongly encouraged to bring their own light source, such as a headlamp or flashlight, as these will not be provided. The course will be marked with glow sticks and reflective arrows to help guide participants through the dark.

  355 Mount Luther Lane, Camp Mount Luther, Mifflinburg, PA

▶ Hair of the Bear 15K/5K — 9 am, Sunday, April 4, Shawnee on Delaware

Scenic race that takes you along the McDade Recreation Trail and finishes with buffet, live music, and craft beers.

  • 5K Course is 90% paved, well marked, and takes place on property and neighboring roads of Shawnee Inn.
  • 15K Course Starts/Finishes at the Brewery. Includes about 5 miles on the beautiful and well maintained McDade Trail system along the Delaware River, plus approximately 4 miles of paved roads.
