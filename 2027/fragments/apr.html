▶ Hair of the Bear 15K/5K — 9 am, Sunday, April 4, Shawnee on Delaware

Scenic race that takes you along the McDade Recreation Trail and finishes with buffet, live music, and craft beers.

  • 5K Course is 90% paved, well marked, and takes place on property and neighboring roads of Shawnee Inn.
  • 15K Course Starts/Finishes at the Brewery. Includes about 5 miles on the beautiful and well maintained McDade Trail system along the Delaware River, plus approximately 4 miles of paved roads.

 ShawneeCraft Brewery located at Shawnee Inn Resort at 1 Shawnee Drive, Shawnee on DE, PA
