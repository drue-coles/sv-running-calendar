▶Muncy Monster Dash — 9:30 am, Saturday, October 30, Muncy

The 5K, timed and untimed, you pick, is for all ages. Strollers are welcomed. No pets, please. There will be route guides along the race’s loop to guide all runners. The Kids’ 1-Mile Fun Run is mainly for students 12 and under. There will be optional obstacles on the first 1-mile loop. Proceeds from this event will be used to support school-wide programs, activities, and supplies offered to the students of Ward L. Myers Elementary School.

  Ward L. Myers Elementary School, 125 New Street, Muncy, PA
