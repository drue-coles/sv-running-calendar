▶The Office 5K — 1 pm, Saturday, May 12, Scranton

The Office 5K is a road race in Scranton, PA that passes sites featured on NBC’s The Office.” Participants can dress up as their favorite Office character for fun and prizes. Walkers can enjoy a modified one mile course.

Awards: There will be about a dozen costume awards, including Best Group Costume, Most Obscure Office Reference, and Best In Show. Top male and female finishers, top male and female masters finishers, and top three finishers in each of the below age groups will receive awards as well.

Proceeds benefit Valley In Motion, a non-profit strengthening sense of community across the Lackawanna Valley.

  Backyard Ale House, 523 Linden Street, Scranton, PA

▶9th annual Tomato Trot 5K — 10 am, Saturday, May 8, Northumberland

This community run/walk benefits local organizations making a difference in the Susquehanna Valley. We are excited to support both The Refuge and Young Life, along with other local youth ministries, as they build relationships with students, provide Christ-centered support, and help young people grow in their faith and purpose.

This family-friendly event is perfect for people of all ages and abilities. Can you beat the Tomato? Participants can race against our running Tomato for bragging rights. Fun activities for kids will be available during the 5K race. The Kids’ Fun Run will occur following the 5K and is free to all kids who would like to participate.

Course: out-and-back course in scenic rolling countryside. Refreshments will be available after the race for all participants.

  Furmano Foods, 770 Cannery Road, Northumberland, PA

▶47th annual Memorial T-Rail 5-Mile Run — 9 am, Saturday, May 29, Danville

This 5-mile course starts and finishes at the Danville Memorial Park. The course features gradual inclines, a couple hills, flats, and plenty of downhills, all on paved roads. Perfect for both beginning and competitive runners! Walkers welcome. Proceeds benefit local community organizations.

  Danville Memorial Park, 300 Bloom Street, Danville, PA
