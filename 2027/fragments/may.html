▶9th annual Tomato Trot 5K — 10 am, Saturday, May 8, Northumberland

This community run/walk benefits local organizations making a difference in the Susquehanna Valley. We are excited to support both The Refuge and Young Life, along with other local youth ministries, as they build relationships with students, provide Christ-centered support, and help young people grow in their faith and purpose.

This family-friendly event is perfect for people of all ages and abilities. Can you beat the Tomato? Participants can race against our running Tomato for bragging rights. Fun activities for kids will be available during the 5K race. The Kids’ Fun Run will occur following the 5K and is free to all kids who would like to participate.

Course: out-and-back course in scenic rolling countryside. Refreshments will be available after the race for all participants.

  Furmano Foods, 770 Cannery Road, Northumberland, PA
