▶SnowFest Trail Race — 1 pm, Sunday, February 21, Mifflinburg

Bundle up and join us for a winter adventure through the woods at our annual 4-Mile SnowFest Trail Race. Whether you're a runner, hiker, or walker, this event welcomes participants of all experience levels and paces. Embrace the crisp winter air, enjoy the scenic trails, and experience the beauty of winter alongside fellow outdoor enthusiasts. After crossing the finish line, head to the pavilion to warm up, rehydrate, and enjoy a delicious selection of hot soups and light refreshments. It's the perfect opportunity to relax, share stories, and connect with fellow trail enthusiasts.

  R.B. Winter State Park, 17215 Buffalo Road, Mifflinburg, PA
